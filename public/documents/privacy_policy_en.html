Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 of January 2025

Introduction

We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This privacy policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

Data Collected

  • Username: A name provided by you within the app.
  • Login token: A random UUID identifier generated by the app and used to distinguish users.

We do not collect or store any additional personal information, such as email addresses or location data.

Use of Data

The collected data is solely used to:

  • Identify users within the app.
  • Save user progress.

We do not share, sell, or distribute your data to third parties.

Data Storage

Your username and login token are securely stored in our database. No other personal data is stored.

Data Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data through the app settings on the 'Passport' page. If you need assistance, please contact us at maxperala@icloud.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. You will be notified by any significant changes.

Contact Us

For questions or concerns about this policy, contact us at maxperala@icloud.com.
